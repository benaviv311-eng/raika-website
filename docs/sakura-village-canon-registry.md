## Publishing protocol
- One location at a time. A location is presented to the creator on the LIVE site for review; no next-location publication until the creator confirms the current location is complete.
- Creator approval applies to the EXACT image, not automatically to other camera angles.
- Track `image_approval` independently from `site_publication`.
- "Continue"/"Next" advances production; does not constitute image approval.
- Rejected images are never published or used to train or reconstruct canon.
- Website/public titles must be English (or explicitly approved original Japanese script).
- Sakura blossom is the civic symbol; lightning belongs only to the Reishin/House Rai tradition.
- No unsolicited change to already approved artworks. Update registry on each publication.
