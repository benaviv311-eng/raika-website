## 1. Locked creative brief — the nature of Sakura Village
A substantial, lived-in ancient Japanese-inspired rabbit village on gentle forested hills, with streams, bridges, gardens, residential areas, training facilities and a bustling craft market. Not a random assortment of disconnected buildings. Every street, bridge, landmark and shop must serve a purpose. Style: polished, warm, cinematic stylized 3D in continuity with RAIKA comic. Coherent building materials, perspective and travel geography.
- **Great Sakura**: grand ancient cherry tree and the village's civic/visual heart; village square, seating and gatherings; visible from principal streets. CENTER-OF-VILLAGE is a locked MASTER-PLAN DIRECTION, with precise position to verify against panels.
- **Sakuranbo School**: Raika's school; classrooms, central schoolyard, sports area and volleyball court, in a quiet residential area connected to the village center.
- **Rai's Aunt's House / Shizuka's House**: quiet, modest ancestral residence with compact garden and shoji entry, near a walkable route from the dojo; candle scene interior with memorial shelf for Kaminari and two candles. Confirm family designation in canon before publishing website labels.
- **Battle Arena**: separate public arena with compacted earth, wooden stands and organized athlete/spectator circulation; NOT the same as day-to-day dojo training yard.
- **Mayor's Residence**: civic leader's substantial but non-royal residence, council hall, reception courtyard, veranda, on a modest rise.
- **Waterways**: a primary stream from the surrounding hills that branches through village and joins again (proposed hydrography); stone central bridge, neighborhood wood bridges, little garden footbridges. Functional irrigation/workshops/gardens and believable paths.
- **Sakura Market**: coherent bustling market quarter with blacksmith/forge at safer outskirts, shoemaker, textile shop, blanket/futon shop, Komugi's bakery, food/vegetable stalls, ceramics, herbs and tea. Consider fire safety and access to stream.
- **Dojo district**: Dojo Kaminari (existing approved entry, courtyard and indoor hall), other training spaces where indicated by comic, with proper distinction from Inazuma dojo if sources distinguish them; no silent merger of distinct canonical places.
- **Residential quarter**: lived-in houses, gardens, public footpaths, everyday village scale.
- **Village exits/outer paths**: verify gate orientation, access to woodland, river and routes to nearby story locations.
