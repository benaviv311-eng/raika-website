03 / The heart of Sakura Village3 approved views

The Great Sakura — Village Square

The ancient cherry blossom tree stands at the heart of Sakura Village. Stone steps, the five-petal blossom mosaic, wooden market stalls and bridges make the square a gathering place for the community. The sakura blossom — not the Reishin lightning — is the village's public symbol.
